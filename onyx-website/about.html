About us

An exclusive lounge, designed for gamers

Onyx VIP Gaming Lounge is a premium gaming venue offering exclusive, high-comfort experiences for people who value privacy, quality equipment and personalised service.


Our name comes from the black gemstone, and our identity follows it: a faceted gem mark, a gold controller and a dark, uncluttered look that keeps the focus on the experience.

Our promise

  • Privacy in every booth
  • Premium, well-maintained equipment
  • A simple, confident booking process
  • A personal touch from our assistants
